## Notes

Why only round 1, rather than both full published schedules: the sandbox
this was assembled in cannot reach a single fixture source. Every host
that carries the data — fotball.no, eliteserien.no, fotmob, worldfootball,
transfermarkt, sofascore, and Wikipedia — is refused by the egress proxy
with a 403 policy denial, so `WebFetch` and `curl` are both dead ends and
web *search* is the only channel left. Search returns clean,
corroborated pairings for the opening round of each league, but past that
the round numbering drifts between queries (one set of eight fixtures came
back labelled "runde 2" in one query and "runde 3" in another) and the
dates stop being quoted at all. Since a schedule with wrong dates scores
as confidently as one with right dates, guessing would have produced a
reference baseline that is worse than no baseline.

What is here is therefore the subset that two or more signals agreed on.
To extend it, run the refresh from a machine with real network access —
`baselines/README.md` has the procedure.

The scoring format is date-only. Kickoff times exist at source (Eliteserien
round 1 ran 16:00 and 18:00 on the Saturday, 14:30 to 19:15 on the Sunday)
but `external_schedule.py` has no column for them, so they are recorded
here rather than silently dropped into a column the loader ignores.
